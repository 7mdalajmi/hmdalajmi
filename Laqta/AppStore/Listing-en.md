## Version 1.0

**Promotional text (170)**

Save photos, screenshots, videos, PDFs and links from any app into beautiful private folders. Laqta reads them on your iPhone, so you find anything in seconds.

**Description (4000)**

Laqta keeps what you save from other apps in calm, beautiful folders, and understands it for you, privately, on your device.

SAVE FROM ANYWHERE
• Share photos, screenshots, videos, screen recordings, PDFs and links to Laqta from any app.
• Laqta suggests the right folder, or a new one, and can remove the original from Photos after it keeps its copy. iOS always asks you first.
• Duplicates are spotted before you save them.
• Import from Photos, Files or the camera document scanner. On iPad, drag items straight into a folder.

UNDERSTANDS WHAT YOU SAVE
• Reads the text in photos, screenshots, video frames and PDFs.
• Finds dates, prices, flights, booking codes, phone numbers and addresses, ready to add to Calendar or open in Maps.
• Recognizes places from a photo's location or from the text, with travel time and directions.
• Summaries and questions with Apple Intelligence, and speech transcripts for your videos, all on device.

LINKS, DONE RIGHT
• Articles, recipes, products, posts and videos keep their title, preview, author and length.
• YouTube videos keep their title, channel and thumbnail, and open in YouTube to watch.

FIND ANYTHING
• Search words inside your shots, videos and PDFs, the places they show, your notes and your links.
• Insights gathers upcoming dates and saved places in one view.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no ads, no analytics. Laqta doesn't collect your data.
• Lock private folders with Face ID.
• Recently Deleted keeps items for 30 days.
• Optional iCloud Sync across your iPhone and iPad, iCloud backups and backup files you can keep anywhere. Everything stays in your own iCloud.

MADE FOR iPHONE AND iPAD
• Noir and Ivory looks, Liquid Glass, and a sidebar layout on iPad.
• Dynamic Type, VoiceOver, Reduce Motion, Reduce Transparency and Increase Contrast support.

## App Privacy

Answer **Data Not Collected**.

Reasons, in case App Review asks:
• Everything is processed and stored on the device, or in the user's own iCloud (CloudKit private database, iCloud Drive).
• There is no developer server, analytics, advertising or third-party SDK.
• Requests that leave the device go directly to the service the user asked for: websites of saved links, Apple Maps (place names), YouTube's oEmbed service (video links only).

## Export compliance

`ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = NO` is set in Info.plist (HTTPS only), so no questions appear on upload.
